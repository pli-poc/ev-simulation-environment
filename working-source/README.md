# EV Simulation Environment — Energy Flexibility Digital Twin

Interactive 3D product-discovery simulator for commercial EV charging, BEMS and energy flexibility.

- `/` — V2 Energy Flexibility Digital Twin
- `/v1/` — preserved V1 Smart Building / BEMS simulator

V2 adds flexibility envelopes, grid-flex commitments, BESS constraints and thermal derating, EV departure constraints, HVAC/cold-store flexibility, telemetry quality, threshold-oriented operator views, fault scenarios, dispatch explanation, and rebound/recovery behaviour.

The model is deliberately a product/operational simulation rather than a protection or three-phase electrical-engineering model. Inputs and scenarios are synthetic.
